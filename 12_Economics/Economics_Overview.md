# Economics — Overview

Two readings. Currency is the heavier, more computational one; growth is conceptual.

## Module 5 — Currency Exchange Rates: Determination and Forecasting
→ [[Currency_Exchange_Rates]]
- Bid-offer spread & drivers; triangular arbitrage
- Forward premium/discount; **forward points & all-in forward rate (÷10,000, JPY ÷100)**; covered IRP forward rate; mark-to-market of a forward
- Parity conditions (covered/uncovered IRP, forward parity, PPP, international Fisher, **real interest rate parity**) and their linkages
- Forecasting & long-run fair value; carry trade (crash risk)
- BOP flows, monetary/fiscal policy (Mundell-Fleming), intervention, capital controls, currency-crisis signs

## Module 6 — Economic Growth
→ [[Economic_Growth]]
- Growth preconditions; stock-market vs GDP-growth link; potential GDP
- Cobb-Douglas production function; growth accounting; labor productivity (two methods to forecast potential GDP)
- Capital deepening vs technological progress (TFP)
- Classical / neoclassical (Solow, `g*=θ/(1−α)`) / endogenous (`Δye/ye = sc−δ−n`) growth theories
- Absolute / conditional / club convergence

## Cross-cutting Exam Traps
- Covered IRP is the only arbitrage-enforced parity; others are tendencies.
- Carry trade profits when UIRP fails, but has negative skew (crash risk).
- Capital deepening cannot sustain per-capita growth; only TFP can (neoclassical).
