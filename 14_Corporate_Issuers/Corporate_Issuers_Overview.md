# Corporate Issuers — Overview

Four modules. Cost of capital and dividends/buybacks are the most computational.

## Module 13 — Dividends and Share Repurchases → [[Dividends_and_Share_Repurchases]]
Wealth/ratio effects; MM/bird-in-hand/tax-aversion theories; signaling, clientele, agency; tax systems;
stable vs constant payout; buyback EPS (E/P vs after-tax cost) and BVPS effects; coverage.

## Module 14 — ESG and Corporate Governance → [[ESG_and_Corporate_Governance]]
Ownership structures (principal-agent vs principal-principal); governance effectiveness; ESG
materiality and integration approaches.

## Module 15 — Cost of Capital: Advanced Topics → [[Cost_of_Capital]]
Top-down/bottom-up factors; cost of debt; ERP (historical vs forward/Grinold-Kroner); required return
(CAPM, expanded CAPM, build-up, bond-yield-plus); country risk premium; beta unlever/relever (Hamada).

## Module 16 — Corporate Restructurings → [[Corporate_Restructurings]]
M&A and divestiture types; valuation methods; takeover premium = (DP − UP)/UP; effects on EPS, net
debt/EBITDA, WACC.

## Cross-cutting Exam Traps
- Buyback accretive if E/P > after-tax cost of funds; BVPS rises only if repurchase price < BVPS.
- Beta: unlever the comparable, relever to the subject (Hamada).
- Takeover premium denominator = pre-announcement unaffected price.
