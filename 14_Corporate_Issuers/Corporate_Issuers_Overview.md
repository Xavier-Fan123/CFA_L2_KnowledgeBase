# Corporate Issuers — Overview

Four modules. Cost of capital and dividends/buybacks are the most computational.

## Module 13 — Dividends and Share Repurchases → [[Dividends_and_Share_Repurchases]]
Wealth/ratio effects; MM/bird-in-hand/tax-aversion theories; signaling, clientele, agency; tax systems; stable vs constant payout; buyback EPS (E/P vs after-tax cost) and BVPS effects; coverage.

## Module 14 — ESG and Corporate Governance → [[ESG_and_Corporate_Governance]]
Ownership (dispersed/principal-agent vs concentrated/principal-principal; horizontal vs vertical/pyramid; dual-class); influential owners; governance effectiveness (board/one-vs-two-tier, CEO duality, remuneration, voting rights); ESG materiality, three information sources (proprietary/vendor/NGO-SASB), integration (equity → opportunities + downside; FI → credit spread).

## Module 15 — Cost of Capital: Advanced Topics → [[Cost_of_Capital]]
Top-down/bottom-up factors; cost of debt (YTM, matrix/synthetic rating, leases, CRR add-on); ERP (historical vs forward — DDM `E(D₁/V₀)+g−Rf` and Grinold-Kroner `[DY+%ΔP/E+i+g−ΔS]−Rf`); required return on equity (DDM, BYPRP, CAPM, **Fama-French 3/5-factor**, expanded CAPM, build-up); private-company premiums **SP/IP/SCRP**; country risk premium; GCAPM/ICAPM; beta unlever/relever (Hamada).

## Module 16 — Corporate Restructurings → [[Corporate_Restructurings]]
Life cycle; three categories (investment: equity-stake/JV/acquisition; divestment: sale/spin-off; restructuring: cost/balance-sheet/reorg) + **LBO**; three-step evaluation (initial — materiality >10% of EV; preliminary — comparable company/transaction/premium-paid + sum-of-parts; modeling — pro forma IS, EPS, net debt/EBITDA, **pro forma WACC**); takeover premium = (DP − SP)/SP.

## Cross-cutting Exam Traps
- Buyback accretive if E/P > after-tax cost of funds; BVPS rises only if repurchase price < BVPS.
- ERP: Grinold-Kroner — higher expected inflation leaves ERP **unchanged** (i added, Rf subtracted).
- Cost of equity (private): **build-up** has NO beta (implicit β=1); **expanded CAPM** uses peer beta.
- Beta: unlever the comparable, relever to the subject (Hamada).
- Restructuring: comparable-**company** multiples need an added premium; comparable-**transaction** embed it.
- Takeover premium denominator = pre-announcement **unaffected** price; pro forma WACC changes weights AND costs.
