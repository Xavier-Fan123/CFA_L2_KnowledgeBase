# Equity Investments — Overview

Six modules, heavily computational. The DCF family (DDM, FCF, residual income) plus multiples is the
core; private company applies it all with discounts.

## Module 17 — Valuation Process → [[Equity_Valuation_Process]]
Intrinsic value & perceived mispricing; going-concern vs liquidation; absolute vs relative;
sum-of-the-parts and conglomerate discount; model selection.

## Module 18 — Dividend Discount Models → [[Dividend_Discount_Models]]
Gordon, PVGO, justified leading/trailing P/E, sustainable growth (g = b×ROE), two-stage, H-model.

## Module 19 — Free Cash Flow → [[Free_Cash_Flow_Valuation]]
FCFF (discount at WACC) vs FCFE (discount at r_e); the full set of formulas; leverage affects FCFE not FCFF.

## Module 20 — Market-Based Valuation → [[Market_Based_Valuation]]
Method of comparables vs justified multiples; P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA; PEG; harmonic mean.

## Module 21 — Residual Income → [[Residual_Income]]
RI = (ROE − r)×B; V0 = B0 + PV(RI); justified P/B = (ROE − g)/(r − g); clean surplus.

## Module 22 — Private Company Valuation → [[Private_Company_Valuation]]
Normalized earnings; build-up/expanded CAPM; income/market/asset approaches; DLOC & DLOM (multiplicative).

## Cross-cutting Exam Traps
- FCFF → WACC; FCFE → cost of equity. Leverage affects FCFE, not FCFF.
- Justified leading P/E = payout/(r−g); justified P/B = (ROE−g)/(r−g).
- Average multiples with the harmonic mean; EV subtracts cash.
- Total private-company discount = 1 − (1−DLOC)(1−DLOM).
