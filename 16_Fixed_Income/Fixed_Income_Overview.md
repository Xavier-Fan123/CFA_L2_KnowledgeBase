# Fixed Income — Overview

Five modules. The tree-based valuation (arbitrage-free + embedded options) and credit (models + CDS) are the heaviest.

## Module 23 — Term Structure → [[Term_Structure]]
Spot/forward/par, bootstrapping, riding the curve, swap spread, TED & MRR−OIS, term-structure theories, yield-curve factors & key-rate duration, and developing rate views from macro factors (bear-flattening / bull-steepening, bullet vs barbell).

## Module 24 — Arbitrage-Free Valuation → [[Arbitrage_Free_Valuation]]
Value additivity; binomial (lognormal) tree, calibration, backward induction, pathwise, Monte Carlo; equilibrium (CIR/Vasicek) vs arbitrage-free (Ho-Lee) models.

## Module 25 — Bonds with Embedded Options → [[Bonds_With_Embedded_Options]]
Callable/putable decomposition; tree valuation (min/max rules); vol & curve effects; OAS; effective duration/convexity; convertibles.

## Module 26 — Credit Analysis Models → [[Credit_Analysis_Models]]
EE, LGD, POD, CVA; rating transition returns; structural vs reduced-form; credit-spread term structure.

## Module 27 — Credit Default Swaps → [[Credit_Default_Swaps]]
Single-name/index CDS; credit events; pricing (upfront ≈ (spread−coupon)×duration); hedging, curve and basis trades.

## Cross-cutting Exam Traps
- If realized rates = forwards, you earn only the risk-free return.
- Higher vol → lower callable, higher putable, lower OAS.
- CVA = PV of expected loss; risky bond = risk-free − CVA.
- CDS payout = notional × (1 − recovery); buyer profits when spreads widen.
