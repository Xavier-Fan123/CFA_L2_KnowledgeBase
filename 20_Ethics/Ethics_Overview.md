# Ethical and Professional Standards — Overview

Level II ethics is **application-driven**: the Code & Standards are identical to Level I, but exam items
are richer case scenarios. The grader rewards the **highest standard of conduct**.

## Modules 40-42 — Code & Standards (and Application) → [[Code_and_Standards]]
Six components of the Code; the **seven Standards** and their subsections; high-frequency application
points (knowledge of the law, independence, MNPI/mosaic theory, fair dealing, loyalty to employer,
priority of transactions); recommended compliance procedures.

## Module 43 — GIPS → [[GIPS]]
Voluntary firm-wide standards for fair performance presentation; composites; key calculation/disclosure
requirements; verification.

## Cross-cutting Exam Traps
- Pick the response requiring the **highest** ethical standard; follow the **stricter** of law vs Code.
- Mosaic theory is allowed; acting on material nonpublic information is not.
- GIPS is **firm-wide, all-or-nothing**; composites must include all discretionary fee-paying portfolios.
